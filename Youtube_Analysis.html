YouTube Trending Videos Analysis¶

Project Overview¶

This project analyzes YouTube trending videos to identify patterns in video performance, audience engagement, title sentiment, publishing country, category, and trending duration.

The analysis combines Python, Pandas, data visualization, sentiment analysis, and SQL to understand what characteristics are associated with higher-performing and longer-trending YouTube videos.

Objectives¶

  • Analyze video performance using views, likes, and comments.
  • Compare video performance across YouTube categories.
  • Compare performance across publishing countries.
  • Analyze sentiment in video titles.
  • Examine the relationship between title sentiment and engagement.
  • Analyze how long videos remain on the trending list.
  • Identify highly trending and highly engaging videos.
  • Use SQL and Python to perform structured data analysis.
  • Generate actionable insights from the results.

query = """
SELECT
    category_id,
    COUNT(DISTINCT video_id) AS video_count,
    ROUND(AVG(views), 2) AS avg_views,
    ROUND(AVG(likes), 2) AS avg_likes,
    ROUND(AVG(comment_count), 2) AS avg_comments
FROM youtube_trending
GROUP BY category_id
ORDER BY avg_views DESC;
"""

category_sql = pd.read_sql_query(query, conn)

df["title_sentiment_score"] = df["title"].apply(
    lambda x: sia.polarity_scores(str(x))["compound"]
)

def classify_sentiment(score):
    if score >= 0.05:
        return "Positive"
    elif score <= -0.05:
        return "Negative"
    else:
        return "Neutral"

df["title_sentiment"] = df["title_sentiment_score"].apply(
    classify_sentiment
)

8. Key Findings¶

The analysis of YouTube trending videos produced the following key findings:

8.1 Category Performance¶

Video performance varies substantially across categories. Differences were observed in average views, likes, comments, and trending duration across category groups.

8.2 Publishing Country¶

Publishing country is associated with differences in both audience performance and trending duration. The analyzed countries showed noticeable variation in average views, likes, comments, and the amount of time videos remained trending.

8.3 Title Sentiment¶

Neutral titles were the dominant sentiment classification, representing 63.24% of the analyzed videos. Positive titles represented 20.06%, while negative titles represented 16.70%.

8.4 Sentiment and Video Performance¶

Negative-title videos had the highest average views, likes, and comments in the analyzed dataset, followed by positive-title videos and neutral-title videos.

This indicates an association between title sentiment and observed video performance, but it does not establish that title sentiment directly causes higher performance.

8.5 Trending Duration¶

Most videos had a short trending duration. The median trending duration was 1 day, while the mean was 2.54 days. A small number of videos remained trending for substantially longer periods, with a maximum observed duration of 205 days.

8.6 Engagement¶

Engagement rates varied considerably between videos. Some videos generated unusually high levels of likes and comments relative to their view counts, indicating strong audience interaction.

8.7 Top Trending Videos¶

A small number of videos had exceptionally long trending periods compared with the majority of the dataset. These videos represent notable examples of sustained visibility on the trending list.

9. Business Insights¶

The findings provide several useful insights for creators, marketers, and content strategists.

9.1 Content Category Strategy¶

Different categories demonstrate different levels of audience reach and engagement. Content creators can use category-level performance patterns as one input when planning future content.

9.2 Title Strategy¶

The analysis shows differences in performance across title-sentiment groups. Titles should therefore be considered as one component of a broader content strategy.

However, title sentiment alone should not be treated as a guarantee of video performance.

9.3 Audience Engagement¶

Views provide an indication of reach, while likes and comments provide additional information about audience interaction. Looking at engagement alongside views gives a more complete picture of video performance.

9.4 Trending Visibility¶

Most videos remain trending for a relatively short period. Videos with unusually long trending durations represent opportunities to investigate additional factors such as topic, channel popularity, timing, and audience interest.

9.5 Country-Level Differences¶

Differences across publishing countries suggest that geographic factors may be relevant when analyzing YouTube content performance. Country should therefore be considered alongside category, title, and engagement metrics.

10. Conclusion¶

This project analyzed YouTube trending videos using Python, Pandas, data visualization, sentiment analysis, and SQL.

The analysis examined video performance, content categories, publishing countries, title sentiment, engagement, and trending duration.

The results showed that:

  • Neutral titles were the most common sentiment classification.
  • Video performance varied considerably across categories and publishing countries.
  • Negative-title videos had the highest average views and engagement measures among the sentiment groups analyzed.
  • Most videos remained trending for only a short period.
  • A small number of videos achieved exceptionally long trending durations.
  • Engagement rate provided an additional perspective beyond raw view counts.

Overall, the project demonstrates how exploratory data analysis, sentiment analysis, and SQL can be combined to identify patterns in large-scale YouTube video data.

The findings represent associations observed in the dataset and should not be interpreted as evidence of direct causation.
